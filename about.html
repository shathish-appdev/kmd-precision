About KMD Precision Products

KMD Precision Products is a precision manufacturing company delivering high-quality plastic and metal components along with end-to-end manufacturing solutions for customers who demand accuracy, consistency, and reliability.

Founded in 2017, KMD Precision Products has established itself as a dependable partner for global OEMs by supplying plastic injection molded parts, stamped components, and precision-machined parts manufactured using CNC and VMC technologies.

We serve a broad range of industries including Automotive & EV, Home Appliances, Medical Equipment, Consumer Electronics, Hydraulics, Oil & Gas, and general industrial OEM applications.

At KMD, we go beyond manufacturing — we collaborate with our customers to deliver engineered solutions that support innovation, efficiency, and long-term success.

Injection Molding & Precision Manufacturing Excellence

KMD Precision Products operates two distinct manufacturing business verticals — Plastic Injection Molding and Precision Machining (CNC & VMC) — each dedicated to delivering specialized, high-quality components for diverse industrial applications.

Plastic Injection Molding

Our Injection Molding division focuses on the manufacture of Engineering and Non-Engineering plastic components, ensuring dimensional accuracy, surface finish, and repeatability. Functional engineering components are developed to meet application-specific performance requirements across industries.

Precision Machining (CNC & VMC)

Our Precision Machining division specializes in CNC and VMC machined metal components, delivering tight tolerances, complex geometries, and consistent quality for demanding engineering applications.
